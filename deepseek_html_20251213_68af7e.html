Marjan's Day

Marjan is a nurse. She works at a big hospital. Her day starts early. She gets up at 5:30 AM every morning.

At work, Marjan helps sick people. She gives them medicine. She talks to the patients. She works with other nurses and doctors.

Marjan has lunch at 1:00 PM. She eats in the hospital cafeteria with her friends from work.

In the evening, Marjan goes home at 7:00 PM. She cooks dinner and sometimes watches TV. She also reads books or calls her friends.

Marjan's job is not a 9-5 job. She works from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM, three days a week. This means she works long hours but has more days off.

Marjan does not see her family a lot during the week because she works long hours. But she visits them on her days off.

To relax, Marjan likes to listen to music and go for walks in the park. On weekends, she sometimes goes to the cinema with friends.

Marjan likes her job very much. She enjoys helping people. The work is hard, but it makes her happy.
